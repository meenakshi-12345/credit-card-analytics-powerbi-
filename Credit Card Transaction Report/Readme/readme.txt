# 💳 Credit Card Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Credit Card Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze transaction performance, customer behavior, spending patterns, and revenue drivers.

The project focuses on turning raw credit card data into actionable business insights that can support customer targeting, marketing decisions, and revenue growth.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Credit card companies generate large volumes of transaction and customer data. However, raw data alone does not provide a clear picture of business performance.

This project analyzes credit card data from two perspectives:

- **Transaction Analysis** – Understanding revenue, transactions, payment methods, card performance, spending categories, and quarterly trends.
- **Customer Analysis** – Understanding customer segments, professions, age groups, marital status, geography, and spending behavior.

The final Power BI dashboard provides an interactive way for business stakeholders to explore these patterns and identify potential growth opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall credit card revenue and transaction performance.
- Identify the best-performing credit card type.
- Understand customer spending behavior.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Identify the highest-performing expenditure categories.
- Compare revenue across states.
- Understand quarterly revenue trends.
- Identify potential customer targeting and revenue-growth opportunities.

## 💡 Key Insights

### Transaction Analysis

- **Blue Card generated the highest revenue**, indicating strong adoption and/or transaction activity.
- **Q3 was the strongest revenue quarter**, while revenue declined in Q4, suggesting possible seasonal or transaction-volume effects.
- **Swipe transactions contributed the largest share of payment revenue**, highlighting strong offline card usage.
- **Bills was the largest expenditure category**, showing the importance of recurring payments in overall card usage.
- **Graduates generated the highest revenue among education segments**, making them an important customer segment to analyze further.

### Customer Analysis

- **Married customers contributed around 51% of total revenue**, making them a major revenue-generating segment.
- **Businessmen emerged as the highest-revenue profession**, indicating a potentially valuable customer segment.
- **Customers aged 40–50 showed the highest card ownership**, highlighting strong adoption among this age group.
- **Texas, New York, and California were among the top-performing states**, indicating strong regional revenue opportunities.
- **Bills remained the largest spending category**, presenting an opportunity for recurring-payment rewards and partnerships.

> Note: Possible reasons behind these patterns are treated as hypotheses and would require deeper analysis of customer count, transaction frequency, and average transaction value for validation.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

### Customer Strategy
- Target high-value customers with personalized offers.
- Encourage high-performing customers to upgrade to premium cards.
- Develop family-oriented rewards for high-value household segments.
- Create dedicated offers for high-value professional segments.

### Revenue Growth
- Use category-specific cashback for high-spending categories.
- Develop rewards around recurring bill payments.
- Investigate the drivers behind the Q3 revenue peak and apply successful strategies to weaker quarters.
- Introduce targeted campaigns to improve Q4 performance.

### Regional Strategy
- Prioritize customer acquisition in high-performing states.
- Analyze successful customer segments in top-performing states.
- Replicate successful strategies in lower-performing markets.

### Payment Strategy
- Continue optimizing physical card/POS usage.
- Introduce incentives to encourage online card transactions.
- Analyze payment behavior by customer segment and card type.
