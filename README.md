## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes credit card customer and transaction data to understand **where revenue comes from, who the most valuable customers are, how customers spend, and which areas represent potential growth opportunities.**

The dashboard is divided into two analytical views:

- **Transaction Report** – Revenue, card performance, payment methods, spending categories, education segments, and quarterly trends.
- **Customer Report** – Customer demographics, professions, age groups, marital status, geographic performance, and spending behavior.

The goal was not just to visualize the data, but to turn the findings into **business insights and actionable recommendations.**

---

##  Key Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which card type generates the highest revenue?
- Which payment method contributes the most revenue?
- Which spending category drives the most revenue?
- Which customer segments are the most valuable?
- Which profession contributes the highest revenue?
- Which age group has the highest card ownership?
- Which states are the strongest revenue-generating markets?
- What factors could be driving quarterly revenue changes?
- Which customer segments should be targeted for premium-card upgrades?

## 💡 Key Insights

### Transaction Analysis

| Observation | Insight | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Card generated the highest revenue | Strong adoption and/or transaction activity may be driving its performance | Retain high-value Blue Card customers and identify premium upgrade opportunities |
| Q3 recorded the highest revenue, followed by a Q4 decline | Seasonal spending or changes in transaction activity may be influencing performance | Analyze Q3 drivers and introduce targeted campaigns during Q4 |
| Swipe transactions contributed the most revenue | Customers continue to rely heavily on physical card payments | Maintain strong POS engagement while encouraging online card usage |
| Bills was the largest expenditure category | Recurring payments represent a major source of card usage | Introduce cashback and rewards for recurring bill payments |
| Graduates generated the highest revenue among education segments | Higher customer representation and/or transaction activity may be contributing | Develop personalized offers for this segment |

###  Customer Analysis

| Observation | Insight | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Married customers contributed ~51% of revenue | Household and recurring expenses may be driving their strong contribution | Introduce family-focused rewards across Bills, Grocery and Fuel |
| Businessmen generated the highest revenue among professions | Higher spending capacity and transaction values may be contributing | Target this segment with premium/business cards and tailored rewards |
| Customers aged 40–50 had the highest card ownership | This represents a strong and financially established customer segment | Focus on retention, loyalty and premium-card upgrades |
| Texas, New York and California were among the top-performing states | Customer volume and/or higher-value spending may be driving regional performance | Prioritize acquisition and targeted campaigns in these markets |
| Bills remained the strongest spending category | Recurring expenses create an opportunity for continued customer engagement | Use cashback and automated-payment rewards to encourage card usage |

> **Note:** Possible reasons are treated as analytical hypotheses and would require deeper analysis of customer count, transaction frequency and average transaction value for validation.
